Qualifying a New Roofing Lead

Sales & Lead Handling · v3.2 · Updated Feb 14, 2026
AI Summary
Every new lead must be contacted within 10 minutes, scored using the AI priority model, and either booked for inspection or moved to nurture within one business day.

Steps

  1. Confirm lead source and contact details are complete in GoHighLevel.
  2. Check the AI priority score generated by the intake automation (Webhook → OpenAI → CRM field update).
  3. Call the homeowner within 10 minutes during business hours; leave a voicemail + auto text if no answer.
  4. If interested, book an inspection directly in the shared calendar and trigger the confirmation sequence.
  5. If not ready, tag as "Nurture" and enroll in the 90-day drip campaign.

CRM Data Entry Standards

CRM & Automation · v2.0 · Updated Jan 30, 2026
AI Summary
Consistent naming, stage usage and required fields keep automations (scoring, routing, reporting) accurate. No deal should move stage without required fields filled.

Steps

  1. Use "Firstname Lastname" format for all contact records — no nicknames or all-caps.
  2. Every deal must have: roof type, estimated value, source, and assigned rep before leaving the "New Lead" stage.
  3. Stage changes must reflect reality the same day — do not batch updates weekly.
  4. Attach CompanyCam project link to the deal record after every site visit.
  5. Lost deals require a lost-reason tag (Price, Timing, Competitor, Unresponsive, Insurance Denied).

n8n Workflow Deployment Checklist

CRM & Automation · v1.6 · Updated Feb 10, 2026
AI Summary
A pre-flight checklist to avoid broken automations in production: test in staging, verify credentials, confirm error workflow, and document the change.

Steps

  1. Build and test the workflow in the staging n8n instance with sample payloads.
  2. Verify all credentials (OpenAI, GoHighLevel, Google Sheets, Slack) are scoped correctly and not expiring soon.
  3. Attach a dedicated Error Workflow that posts failures to the #automation-alerts Slack channel.
  4. Add a short description and tag the workflow with owner + last-reviewed date.
  5. Promote to production during low-traffic hours and monitor the execution log for 24 hours.

Handling Insurance Claim Escalations

Customer Service · v2.3 · Updated Feb 2, 2026
AI Summary
When a homeowner reports a denied or delayed insurance claim, escalate to the estimator within 4 hours and keep the customer informed at every step.

Steps

  1. Log the escalation as a high-priority task assigned to the estimator on file.
  2. Request claim documentation from the homeowner (denial letter, adjuster report).
  3. Estimator reviews scope vs. adjuster estimate and prepares a supplement package if justified.
  4. Office manager schedules a call with the homeowner within 24 hours to explain next steps.
  5. Track resolution status weekly until claim is closed.

Storm Damage Inspection Protocol

Field Operations · v1.9 · Updated Jan 18, 2026
AI Summary
Standardized photo documentation and measurement process so every estimate is backed by verifiable evidence for both the homeowner and the insurance adjuster.

Steps

  1. Photograph all four roof slopes plus close-ups of any hail bruising or wind damage.
  2. Upload all photos to CompanyCam immediately and tag the associated CRM deal.
  3. Measure roof square footage and note pitch, layers, and any decking issues.
  4. Complete the digital inspection form before leaving the property.
  5. Notify the assigned rep that inspection is complete so the estimate workflow can start.

Post-Install Review Request Sequence

Customer Service · v1.4 · Updated Feb 5, 2026
AI Summary
Automated but human-monitored: review requests go out 3 days after job completion, with any rating below 4 stars routed directly to the office manager same-day.

Steps

  1. Job is marked "Complete" in GoHighLevel by the production crew lead.
  2. Automation waits 3 days, then sends an email + SMS review request with direct Google review link.
  3. If no response after 5 days, a single reminder is sent.
  4. Any rating below 4 stars triggers an immediate Slack alert to the office manager for personal follow-up.

Onboarding a New Sales Rep

Onboarding · v2.1 · Updated Jan 12, 2026
AI Summary
A 5-day ramp covering CRM access, pipeline stages, estimating software, and shadowing two live inspections before taking solo leads.

Steps

  1. Day 1: CRM + email + Slack access provisioned; review CRM Data Entry Standards SOP.
  2. Day 2: Walkthrough of pipeline stages, lead scoring, and task assignment logic.
  3. Day 3: Shadow an experienced rep on 2 live calls and 1 inspection.
  4. Day 4: Practice estimate building and quote sending in a sandbox account.
  5. Day 5: Take first solo lead with manager review of the full deal cycle.

Quote & Contract Approval Workflow

Admin & Finance · v1.3 · Updated Jan 25, 2026
AI Summary
Quotes above $30,000 require estimator + owner sign-off before sending; all others can be sent directly by the assigned rep.

Steps

  1. Rep builds the quote in the estimating tool using current material pricing.
  2. Quotes under $30,000 are sent directly to the homeowner by the rep.
  3. Quotes at or above $30,000 are routed to the estimator and owner for approval first.
  4. Once approved, contract is generated for e-signature and logged to the CRM automatically.

Slack Alerting Standards for Automations

CRM & Automation · v1.1 · Updated Feb 8, 2026
AI Summary
Keep signal-to-noise high: only route time-sensitive or exception events to Slack, and always include a direct CRM link in the alert.

Steps

  1. Route only exceptions and time-sensitive events (errors, high-priority leads, low reviews) to Slack.
  2. Routine confirmations (e.g. every successful sync) stay in the execution log only.
  3. Every alert must include a direct link back to the relevant CRM record.
  4. Review channel noise monthly and mute/adjust rules that are ignored.

Material Ordering & Job Scheduling

Field Operations · v1.7 · Updated Feb 1, 2026
AI Summary
Materials are ordered as soon as a contract is signed, with delivery timed 2 days ahead of the scheduled crew start date.

Steps

  1. Trigger material list generation from the approved quote line items.
  2. Place supplier order within 24 hours of contract signature.
  3. Confirm delivery date lands at least 2 business days before crew start.
  4. Add job to the production calendar and notify the crew lead via Slack.
