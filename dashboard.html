Automation running in this CRM

Storm-damage lead auto-scoring
Every inbound form submission is analyzed by OpenAI, scored for priority (weather event, insurance coverage, roof age) and routed to the right rep in under 10 seconds.
Missed call → text-back
A missed inbound call automatically triggers a personalized SMS within 60 seconds, so no storm lead goes cold.
Contract signed → crew notified
When a contract is e-signed in GoHighLevel, n8n logs the job to Google Sheets, creates a CompanyCam project, and pings the production Slack channel.
Post-install review requests
Three days after a job is marked complete, an automated review-request sequence goes out by email and SMS, with negative-rating alerts routed to the office manager.
