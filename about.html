About

I connect the tools your team already uses — so nothing falls through the cracks.

I'm an AI Automation and CRM & Workflow Optimization Specialist based in Valencia, Spain, working with businesses across roofing, construction, property management and service industries.

My background is in business operations — configuring CRMs, coordinating teams and projects, documenting processes and improving how work actually gets done day to day. That operational grounding is what shapes the systems I build now: I design AI workflows, CRM implementations and internal tools that fit how a team already works, rather than forcing a team to adapt to a rigid new system.

In practice, that means fewer dropped leads, less time spent on manual data entry, and processes that survive turnover because they're documented and partly automated instead of living in one person's head.

How I got here

From running operations to automating them

Business Operations & Project Coordination

Managed day-to-day operations, coordinated cross-functional teams, and kept projects moving across scheduling, communication and reporting.

CRM Configuration & Optimization

Set up and refined CRM systems — pipelines, custom fields, automations and reporting — so sales and operations teams had one reliable source of truth.

Process Documentation

Documented recurring processes into SOPs the team could actually follow, reducing the "how do I do this again?" questions.

Workflow & Automation Focus

Moved into building the automations themselves — n8n workflows and AI integrations that took over the repetitive parts of those documented processes.
